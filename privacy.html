Forge Survivor Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-30

What we collect

  • On-device storage: progress, gear and settings stay in your browser storage (localStorage) on this device. They are not sent to a server.
  • Anonymous device key: for friend-invite rewards, a randomly generated device key is sent to our invite server (forge-invite.remin1994.workers.dev). We do not collect names, phone numbers or emails. Request IPs are used only for per-minute rate limiting.
  • Usage analytics: Google Analytics 4 receives anonymous events (app open, run start/end, purchase attempts, errors), a random client ID, screen size and language. Not used for ad personalization.
  • Payments: handled by Toss in-app payment or the app store. We never receive card or bank details. Only an order ID and product name are recorded on the device to confirm purchases.
  • Ads: when you watch a rewarded ad, the ad provider may process ad-viewing data.

How we use it

  • Saving progress, restoring purchases, invite rewards, bug fixing and balancing only.
  • We do not sell it or share it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

  • On-device data disappears when you clear browser data.
  • To delete anonymous server or analytics data, email us with your device key.

Children

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.
