수집하는 정보

  • 기기 저장: 진행 상황, 장비, 설정은 이 기기에 저장합니다.
  • 익명 기기 키와 서버 저장: 친구 초대 보상, 서버 저장, 주간 랭킹을 위해 무작위로 만든 기기 키와 닉네임 태그, 점수를 우리 서버(forge-invite.remin1994.workers.dev)로 보냅니다. 이름·전화번호·이메일은 받지 않습니다. 요청 IP는 분당 횟수 제한에만 씁니다.

  • 결제: Google Play 결제로 처리됩니다. 카드·계좌 정보는 우리가 받지 않습니다.
  • 알림: 기기 안에서 예약하는 로컬 알림만 쓰며, 알림 권한은 언제든 기기 설정에서 끌 수 있습니다.

No Way, It Works Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-31

What we collect

  • On-device storage: progress, gear and settings are stored on your device.
  • Anonymous device key and server save: for friend-invite rewards, server save and the weekly ranking, a randomly generated device key, a nickname tag and your score are sent to our server (forge-invite.remin1994.workers.dev). We do not collect names, phone numbers or emails. Request IPs are used only for per-minute rate limiting.
  • Analytics and crash logs: Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics receive app activity (app open, run start/end, purchase attempts, errors), device identifiers (app instance ID), device model and OS version, and crash logs. Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Ads: to show Google AdMob rewarded ads, the advertising ID, device information and ad-viewing data are shared with Google. You can reset or turn off the advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Payments: handled by Google Play. We never receive card or bank details.
  • Notifications: only local notifications scheduled on your device; you can turn them off any time in device settings.

How we use it

  • Saving progress, restoring purchases, invite rewards, ranking, showing ads, bug fixing and balancing only.
  • We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with third parties other than the Google services listed above.

Retention and deletion

  • On-device data disappears when you uninstall the app.
  • To delete anonymous server or analytics data, email us the address below with the device key shown in the in-app settings. We delete it promptly after verification.

Children

  • The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
