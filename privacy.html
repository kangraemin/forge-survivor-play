수집하는 정보

  • 게임 저장: 진행 상황, 장비, 설정과 구매 복원 정보는 기기의 localStorage 및 앱 저장소에 저장합니다. 스토어 앱과 앱인토스에서는 복구와 기기 이전을 위해 기기 키와 저장 사본을 Cloudflare 서버(forge-invite.remin1994.workers.dev)로 전송합니다. 웹 체험판은 기본적으로 기기에만 저장합니다.
  • 기기 키: 저장 복구와 친구 초대 보상에 쓰는 키를 서버(forge-invite.remin1994.workers.dev)로 보냅니다. 앱인토스에서는 토스가 제공하는 사용자 해시 키를 우선 사용하고, 사용할 수 없거나 다른 플랫폼이면 기기에서 무작위로 생성합니다. 이름·전화번호·이메일은 받지 않습니다. 남용 방지를 위해 요청 IP를 분당 횟수 제한에 씁니다.

  • 결제: 토스 인앱결제 또는 스토어 결제로 처리됩니다. 카드·계좌 정보는 우리가 받지 않습니다. 주문 번호와 상품 정보는 구매 확인·복원을 위해 게임 저장 데이터에 기록하며 서버 저장 사본에도 포함될 수 있습니다.

What we collect

  • Game storage: progress, gear, settings and purchase recovery information are stored locally. Store apps and Apps in Toss also send a device key and save backups to our Cloudflare server (forge-invite.remin1994.workers.dev) for recovery and device transfer. The web demo stores progress locally by default.
  • Device key: a key used for save recovery and friend-invite rewards is sent to our server (forge-invite.remin1994.workers.dev). Apps in Toss prefers a user hash key provided by Toss; when unavailable or on other platforms, a key is randomly generated on the device. We do not collect names, phone numbers or emails. Request IPs are used for per-minute rate limiting.
  • Weekly leaderboards: we store the device key, tower record and weapon enhancement score on our server. Other players can see a pseudonymous tag derived from the key, score and rank.
  • Analytics and diagnostics: the web version sends usage events, a random client ID, screen size and language to Google Analytics 4. Store apps use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to process app activity, app/device identifiers, device information and error reports, and Remote Config to retrieve app settings.
  • Payments: handled by Toss in-app payment or the app store. We never receive card or bank details. Order IDs and product information needed for purchase confirmation and restoration are recorded in game saves and may be included in server backups.
  • Ads: store apps use Google AdMob; Apps in Toss uses Toss rewarded ads. Ad providers may process advertising identifiers (depending on settings and consent), IP addresses, device information and ad impressions/interactions for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. See the Google Privacy Policy.

How we use it

  • Saving progress, restoring purchases, invite rewards, bug fixing and balancing.
  • We do not sell personal information. The storage, analytics and ad providers described above process information to provide their respective services.

Retention and deletion

  • Local data disappears when you clear app or browser data. Server backups and analytics records may remain separately.
  • To request deletion of server or analytics data, email us with your device key.

Children

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.
